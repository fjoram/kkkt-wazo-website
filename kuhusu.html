Kuhusu usharika wetuAbout our parish

Familia ya waumini wa Kilutheri inayokutana Wazo, Dar es Salaam, kumwabudu Mungu, kujifunza Neno lake na kuhudumiana.A Lutheran family of believers gathering in Wazo, Dar es Salaam, to worship God, learn His Word and serve one another.

Historia yetuOur history

  1. [mwaka]Mwanzo mdogoA small beginning

    Waumini wachache walianza kukutana [mahali] kama mtaa wa kuhubiria.A few believers began meeting at [place] as a preaching point.

  2. [mwaka]Usharika kamiliA full parish

    Kwa neema ya Mungu mtaa ulikua na kuzinduliwa rasmi kuwa usharika.By God's grace the preaching point grew and was officially inaugurated as a parish.

  3. LeoTodayUsharika unaokuaA growing parish

    Waumini zaidi ya [idadi] na mitaa 50, tukihudumia Wazo na maeneo jirani kupitia ibada, vikundi na huduma kwa jamii.More than [number] members and 50 preaching points, serving Wazo and nearby areas through worship, fellowships and community service.

TunachoaminiWhat we believe
