Neno la MchungajiA word from our pastor
“Karibu nyumbani kwa Bwana. Iwe ni mara yako ya kwanza au umekuwa nasi kwa miaka mingi, kuna nafasi kwako katika familia hii ya imani. Njoo tumwabudu Mungu pamoja.”“Welcome to the house of the Lord. Whether it is your first time or you have been with us for many years, there is a place for you in this family of faith. Come and worship God with us.”
